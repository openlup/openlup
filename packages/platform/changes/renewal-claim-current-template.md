- Fixed a billing defect in the node-postgres renewal rail. In Platform 1.3.x, a v1 renewal snapshot built before an accepted bundle edit and first claimed after it could charge the pre-edit amount. With the forward installed, version evidence that proves the snapshot stale causes a first claim to return `subscription_renewal_snapshot_stale`. Bare legacy claims without build-version evidence are admitted as before.

Migration:

- Follow the renewal checklist in the database upgrade guide. Before the forward, pause and drain renewal claim creation, renewal and retry dispatch, including custom senders, and template-edit acceptance. Keep claims and template edits paused through forward commit and keep verified evidence intake available. Reconcile listed renewal work and late-arrival schedules. Immediately before resuming dispatch, confirm that the alignment check is empty and that any remaining renewal rows list only `provider_state_open_after_cancellation`. Custom legacy callers build and claim in one transaction that holds the subscription row lock. Re-read and rebuild on `subscription_renewal_snapshot_stale`.
