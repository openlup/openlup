The native publication recorder verifies saved basis-aware price edits and applies fresh edits atomically with issuance. Owner-only assessment and locked readback use the same decision routine. Assessment gives advisory feedback on the proposed approval seal. The locked reader remains dormant. A later change will connect Prepare, add its read-only snapshot mode, and grant runtime access.

Issuance and locked readback require `READ COMMITTED READ WRITE`. A SKU gaining an effective selling mode or entering its first admitted offer needs a fresh price edit in that issuance.

Receipt verification uses the approved settlement context and locked price history. If a receipt's current entry has changed or expired, reload the effective price and submit a fresh edit.
