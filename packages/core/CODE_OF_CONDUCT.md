# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want participation in this project to be respectful, specific, and
constructive. Examples of welcome behavior include:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people,
- giving actionable feedback and accepting correction with grace,
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and skill levels,
- protecting private information and allowing others to set personal
  boundaries,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm where possible.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and project-run community spaces. It also applies when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context,
severity, prior behavior, impact, and the safety of affected people.

## Reporting

The repository-root `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` owns conduct reporting and enforcement.
The repository-root `SECURITY.md` owns vulnerability reporting. This packaged
copy defines no separate moderator, inbox, or fallback reporting route.

Do not put private, sensitive, or identifying incident details in a public
issue. Follow the currently authorized repository-root policies.
